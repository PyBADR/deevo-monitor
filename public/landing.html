Everything You Need

Built for insurance professionals, risk analysts, and intelligence teams in the Gulf region.

🗺️
Interactive GCC Map
2D/3D map with 26 toggleable data layers — claims heatmaps, fraud hotspots, oil facilities, shipping lanes, conflict zones, and insurance asset clusters.
📰
Live News Aggregation
45+ RSS feeds from Reuters ME, Al Arabiya, Bloomberg, SAMA, Artemis, GDACS — filtered by channel with real-time alert classification.
🧠
AI Insights via Ollama
Local AI generates GCC briefs, insurance forecasts, and intel synthesis. No API keys, no cloud dependency. Your data stays on your machine.
📊
Country Risk Scoring
24-signal composite index across 6 categories (U/C/S/I/E/F) for all GCC nations. Real-time trend tracking with insurance-specific weighting.
⚡
Strategic Posture
5 theater assessments — Iran, Yemen/Red Sea, Arabian Gulf, Horn of Africa, Levant — with CRIT/HIGH/ELEV/NORM classification.
🛡️
Insurance-Specific Layers
Claims heatmaps, fraud detection zones, insured asset clusters, reinsurance treaty boundaries, and loss accumulation tracking.
🛢️
Infrastructure Mapping
15 oil & gas facilities, 9 major ports, desalination plants, mega projects (NEOM), and undersea cable routes with insured values.
📹
Live Webcams
14 live streams from GCC cities, ports, and critical infrastructure — Riyadh, Dubai, Makkah, Jebel Ali, Strait of Hormuz.
🌐
Bilingual AR/EN
Full Arabic RTL support with native-language news feeds, layer names, and country data. Toggle between English and Arabic instantly.

Production Architecture

Built on proven patterns from World Monitor (42k+ stars). Optimized for GCC insurance intelligence.

LayerTechnology
FrontendVanilla TypeScript, Vite 6, MapLibre GL, deck.gl, Chart.js
Map EngineMapLibre GL (2D) + globe.gl / Three.js (3D)
AI / MLOllama (local), Groq (cloud fallback), Transformers.js (browser)
Data Feeds45+ RSS, ACLED, GDACS, NASA FIRMS, AIS, USGS
Caching3-tier: In-memory → Redis → Upstream API
DesktopTauri 2 (Rust) — macOS, Windows, Linux
BackendNode.js API server + Deevo Analytics Cortex bridge
DeploymentVercel Edge Functions / Docker / self-hosted
